# 2026–2028 executable research roadmap — CAPE-Graph

## 2026: sound benchmark-oracle foundations (implemented prototype + next gates)

**Available now:** CAPE-Plan v0.2 budgeted conjunctive evidence acquisition, exact branch-and-bound with independent exhaustive validation on 363 synthetic configurations, alongside the verified causal-provenance/permission graph, strict issuer-aware evidence binding, three-valued decisions, counterexample isolation, positive-outcome grading and three synthetic use cases. Tested with 71 framework unit tests, and an independent 42-case negative mutation matrix. The previously implemented domain-level prototypes independently passed 97 more CPU tests.

**Not yet achieved:** official PALE plan approval, first merged Task, official GPT/Claude/Gemini fresh-state rollouts, held-out false-positive and false-negative calibration, integration with actual Infra event shapes. No top-conference or accepted-paper claim.

**Next measurable exit gate:** one task-specific CAPE adapter running against a permitted official Task implementation, preserving the reviewed rubric; reference completion=full points, no-op=0, and 100% of agreed red-team negatives rejected *without excluding paraphrased valid agent strategies*.

## 2027: Counterexample-Guided Evidence Contract Synthesis (research hypothesis)

Introduce **CECS**, an algorithm that (i) extracts candidate state invariants from natural-language rubrics and operational schemas; (ii) uses a symbolic constraint solver and counterexample-guided refinement to search for an *unintended path to a high score*; (iii) requests human/maintainer review of smallest counterexamples; and (iv) emits a regression and updated contract. Policy review stays explicit; LLM-generated safety rules are never self-authorizing.

**Research hypotheses:** (H1) CECS finds more genuinely novel evaluator faults per person-hour than handcrafted test mutation, (H2) explicit positive-outcome and authority constraints reduce reward hacking while preserving safe alternative strategies, (H3) counterfactual history interventions expose failures invisible to final-state-only scoring.

**Required experiments:** independent held-out tasks across at least three App categories and distinct authors, controlled comparison to state-only/proxy-state/judge baselines, quantitative unsafe acceptance, valid paraphrase rejection, annotation agreement and resource costs. 2027 is a target horizon, **not a forecast of acceptance**.

## 2028: Proof-Carrying Verifiable Reward for Adaptive Agents (research hypothesis)

Study a **proof-carrying reward interface** for reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards: successful actions include independently checkable issuer-bound witness references; blocked or genuinely unknown outcomes receive differentiated credit and uncertainty penalties; reward policies adapt to new Apps through versioned contracts without rewriting historical evaluation traces. Separate monitor uncertainty from agent incompetence.

**Research hypotheses:** (H4) proof-aware reward avoids oracle exploitation relative to state-only reward in unseen environments, (H5) explicit abstention on unobservable outcomes improves risk-coverage trade-offs, and (H6) selective verification can reduce cost without increasing false acceptance under a predeclared bound.

**Required experiments:** paired training/evaluation settings on held-out apps, strict agent and grader isolation, native Apps vs simulated services, robustness to task/rubric changes, latency/memory benchmarking, distribution shift and counterfactual invariance. No claim of achieved 2028 algorithm or learned improvement is made today.
